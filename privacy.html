Paper Rocket – Privacy Policy

Developer: conspiracy labs · Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Paper Rocket ("the game") handles, and why. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to do that.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your high-score table (distance and date) and your sound setting in a file on your device. This never leaves your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows occasional full-screen ads between runs, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be inferred,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We (conspiracy labs) do not receive any of this information about you personally.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): when you first start the game, you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change that choice at any time: tap the gear on the title screen, then the padlock button.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game removes everything it stored. For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. Requests about the advertising data Google holds should go to Google, via the links above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Things the game does not do

No accounts or sign-in, no analytics or tracking by us, no in-game chat, and no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
